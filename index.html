Personal project, 2026

Google Earth Engine, Sentinel-2, CHIRPS, Rasterio, xarray

Kolar Dam Surface Water Change Detection (2019–2025)

Measured surface water fluctuations of Kolar Dam from cloud-masked Sentinel-2 composites every May, using an NDWI thresholding baseline.

Findings
Quantified surface shrinkage to 4.86 km² in 2019 compared to 13.2–15.4 km² in 2021–2025. Proved surface area strongly correlates with the preceding year's monsoon total rather than same-year rainfall.
Corrections
Redrew AOI bounds to prevent clipping reservoir branches and re-computed local planar surface areas under UTM Zone 43N reprojection.
