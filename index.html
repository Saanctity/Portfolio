Computer vision and deep learning

Personal project, 2026

PyTorch, Google Earth Engine, scikit-learn

U-Net water segmentation on satellite imagery

A 117K-parameter U-Net written from scratch in PyTorch (encoder, bottleneck, and a decoder with skip connections), with custom DataLoaders for multi-band input and a combined binary cross-entropy and Dice loss. Trained on five Sentinel-2 patches around Kolar Dam and tested on Bhopal's Upper Lake, which it never saw during training.

Result
IoU 0.65 and F1 0.79 against an NDWI water mask on the held-out lake.
Limits
Labels come from NDWI, so the score measures agreement with that formula. Five training patches, one test lake.

Personal project, 2026

Google Earth Engine, Sentinel-2, CHIRPS, Rasterio, xarray, PyProj

Kolar Dam water-change study, 2019 to 2025

Measured the surface water of Kolar Dam near Bhopal from cloud-masked Sentinel-2 composites each May, using an NDWI water mask.

Finding
4.86 square kilometres in 2019 against 13.2 to 15.4 in 2021, 2023 and 2025. Comparing each May with the previous year's rainfall explained the 2019 low; same-year rainfall pointed the wrong way.
Fixes
My first area of interest clipped the reservoir's branching shape, so I redrew it over the whole water body. I re-did the area calculation locally after reprojecting from degrees to UTM zone 43N.
Limits
Four dates, one reservoir, no gauge data yet, and rainfall averaged over my area box rather than the full catchment.
